Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Information we process

Auramem processes account details, caregiver-provided profile and memory information, chat messages needed to provide the companion, and structured activity events. We do not sell personal information.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, personalize Aura, display saved memories, provide games, and generate informational activity summaries. Activity summaries are not medical diagnoses.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication and data storage. Groq processes chat requests when the chat feature is used. Requests are made over HTTPS using server-side credentials.

Retention and deletion

You can request deletion from the caregiver dashboard. Deletion removes the account and associated application records when the required Supabase administrative configuration is enabled.

Security and contact

Use a strong password and report suspected account or privacy issues to the operator responsible for your Auramem deployment. This policy should be reviewed with your organization before production use.
